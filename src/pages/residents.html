For residents

Resident center

Request a repair, find what to do in an emergency, and get answers about rent and your lease.

Emergency? Fire, gas smell or anyone in danger: leave the home and call 911. For gas, call Columbia Gas at 1-800-344-4077 from outside. Flooding, no heat in winter or a burst pipe: call the emergency number in your lease right away, then submit the form below.

Repairs

Request a repair

Describe the problem and add a photo if you can. We confirm we received it and contact you to schedule the work.

  • One request per problem helps us fix things faster
  • A photo or short video saves a trip
  • Tell us when it's OK to come in

Paying rent

Pay the way described in your lease. Online rent payment is coming soon — we'll let every resident know when it's ready.

Your lease

Questions about your lease, renewal or moving out? Send us a message and we'll reply within one business day.

Moving out

Please give written notice as set out in your lease. We'll send a move-out checklist to help get your deposit back.
